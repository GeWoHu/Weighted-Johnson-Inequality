# Known limitations (author-admitted)

1. **Method scope.** The deletion induction is specific to the Boolean
   lattice level structure (exactly two children with the same ambient
   parameters; the perfect-square factorization depends on the constants
   `C(m, k)`). No analogous result is claimed for other graded posets.
2. **Spectral scope.** Only the top eigenvalue of `G_k(a) = U_k(a)U_k(a)ᵀ`
   is controlled, not the full weighted spectrum.
3. **Novelty.** The equal-weight ray is classical and cited. The weighted
   sharp bound with its equality classification surfaced in no surveyed
   source (association schemes, inclusion matrices, differential posets,
   reverse hypercontractivity, polarization constants), but full-text
   MathSciNet-level checks of four adjacent works remain open. Current
   classification: **PLAUSIBLY NEW — SEARCH INCOMPLETE**. No originality
   claim is made from keyword-search absence alone.
4. **No RH claim.** Connections to Riemann-hypothesis-type operator
   programs (e.g. Nyman–Beurling formulations) are historical motivation
   only; any such connection is conditional and unproved, and the paper
   makes no claim about the Riemann Hypothesis.
5. **Review status.** The proof passed independent adversarial AI review
   with zero mathematical objections; independent human peer review has
   not yet been performed. Finite computational checks are regression
   evidence, not part of the proof.
