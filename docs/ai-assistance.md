# AI assistance disclosure

Research development, proof search, symbolic and numerical verification,
literature screening, and manuscript preparation for this repository were
performed with substantial assistance from an AI research agent (LLM-based,
2026), under human direction.

Roles AI performed:

- **Research development:** formulation of the weighted up-operator
  problem; deletion-induction proof search; equality-case analysis;
  identification and verification of the squarefree arithmetic compression
  application (Section 6).
- **Proof generation:** the written proof in `paper/weighted-johnson.tex`
  was drafted by the AI agent and checked step by step; every identity it
  relies on is stated in closed form in the paper.
- **Verification:** general-symbolic checks (sympy, general parameters)
  and independent from-the-definition regression batteries
  (`verification/`) were produced and run by the AI agent.
- **Adversarial review:** multiple isolated AI referee rounds were run,
  including a fresh reviewer given no prior project conclusions. These are
  adversarial self-checks, **not** peer review.

The human author (name to be supplied before submission) takes full
responsibility for all claims in this repository. No AI system is
presented as an author, and no AI review is presented as peer review.
Independent human mathematical review and complete novelty verification
(MathSciNet level) remain pending.
